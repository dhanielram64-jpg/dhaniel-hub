Cognosphere Statement on U.S. FTC Settlement

Company 01/18/2025

SINGAPORE, Jan 18, 2025 - Cognosphere, the distributor of Genshin Impact, has reached a settlement agreement with the United States Federal Trade Commission to strengthen and increase transparency around our parental consent and virtual currency practices. A statement from Cognosphere is below:

"Animation-style games and shows are well-received by global audiences and players across various ages. Genshin Impact is a popular free-to-play, anime-style game designed for older teens and adults. While we believe many of the FTC's allegations are inaccurate, we agreed to this settlement because we value the trust of our community and share a commitment to transparency for our players. Under the agreement, we will introduce new age-gate and parental consent protections for children and young teens and increase our in-game disclosures around virtual currency and rewards for players in the U.S. in the coming months."

May 7, 2025 Update - Introducing New Age Gate, Parental Controls, and Drop Rate Disclosure in Genshin Impact for All U.S. Players

In line with the agreement with the United States Federal Trade Commission, we will be introducing the Age Gate, Parental Controls, and updating Drop Rate disclosures with more details for players residing in the U.S.

• Age Gate & Parental Controls

By May 20, we will roll out the new Age Gate and certain functions of the Parental Control system in Genshin Impact and HoYoLAB, the community forum for HoYoverse games, for all players residing in the U.S. We strongly recommend that all affected players verify their age as soon as possible to avoid the risk of account suspension and deletion of all HoYoverse data.

U.S. players verified as younger players will need consent from a parent or guardian to continue accessing Genshin Impact or HoYoLAB. Once consent is provided, parents and guardians can manage younger players' access to Genshin Impact, its in-game community, and HoYoLAB through the new Parental Control Portal. Parental controls for payment access in Genshin Impact will be available in July.

Learn more on our Minor Safety Center.

• Wish Drop Rate Notice

In addition, we have updated the disclosure of the Wish Drop Rate in Genshin Impact. For further information, please check in-game or visit the Wish Drop Rate Notice on the official Genshin Impact website.

Similar functions will be implemented in other HoYoverse titles, with further progress to be shared in the future.

May 21, 2025 Update - Minor Safety Protection and Warp Rate Disclosure in Honkai: Star Rail for U.S. Players

By June 9, we will introduce the Age Gate and Parental Controls in Tears of Themis for all players residing in the U.S. Following the implementation, U.S. players verified as younger players will need consent from a parent or guardian to continue accessing the game, and in-game social features will remain unavailable until they meet the age requirement. Parental controls for payment access will be available in July, 2025. For more details, please visit our Minor Safety Center.

Additionally, we have updated the Tears of Themis Card Probability Distribution notice on the official Tears of Themis website and in-game.

June 26, 2025 Update - Minor Safety Protection and Supply Drop Rate Disclosure in Honkai Impact 3rd for U.S. Players

In line with compliance requirements for protecting younger players' privacy and online safety in the U.S., we will introduce the Age Gate and Parental Controls in Honkai Impact 3rd for players residing in the U.S. Following the implementation, separate consents will be required for younger U.S. players to access the game and use its in-game social features. Additionally, parental controls for payment access will be available starting in July 2025.

We've also updated the Supply Drop Rate disclosure in Honkai Impact 3rd. For further details, please check in-game or visit the official website.

June 30, 2025 Update – New Age Gate, Parental Controls in Zenless Zone Zero for U.S. Players

In accordance with U.S. compliance requirements for the privacy protection and online safety of younger players, we will be introducing the Age Gate and Parental Controls in Zenless Zone Zero for players residing in the United States. Once implemented, U.S. players identified as minors will be required to obtain separate consents to access the game and use in-game social features. In addition, Parental Controls for payment access will become available starting in July 2025.

Furthermore, we have updated the disclosure of Signal Search Rate in Zenless Zone Zero. For more details, please check in-game or visit the Signal Search Rate Notice on the official Zenless Zone Zero website.
