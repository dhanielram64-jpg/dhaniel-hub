HoYoverse | Extraordinary Starts With You - Celebrating International Women's Day 2023

Company 03/08/2023

Happy International Women's Day!

HoYoverse is proud to join the celebration on this important day to commemorate the achievements of women around the world. Today, we are thrilled to ask some of our outstanding colleagues, who all come from different parts of the world and excel in various roles, to share their stories and experiences as women in the gaming industry. If you are interested in joining the gaming industry, we invite you to listen to their accounts, because "extraordinary starts with you."
